MYG-351: LAST SIGNAL

ES: Simulador de gestión de 14 días, en 2D y para un jugador, ambientado en la misión lunar Artemis-MYG (2032). Eres Elena, comandante de la nave Orión, y debes traer de vuelta a cuatro astronautas. Antes del despegue repartes 10.000 créditos entre dos paquetes de equipamiento; cada día la nave consume recursos y resuelves dos decisiones con tiempo límite. Hay 65 decisiones y 3 finales.

EN: A 14-day, single-player 2D management simulator set in the Artemis-MYG lunar mission (2032). You are Elena, commander of the Orion spacecraft, and must bring four astronauts home. Before launch you split 10,000 credits between two equipment packages; every day the ship consumes resources and you make two timed decisions. There are 65 decisions and 3 endings.

Engine: Godot 4.x (GDScript)
Languages: English (default) and Spanish, switchable from the main menu
Run: open the project in Godot 4 and press Play (main scene: scenes/main_menu.tscn)
